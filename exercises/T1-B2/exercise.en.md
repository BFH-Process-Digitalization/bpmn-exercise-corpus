# T1-B2 Goods receipt at a distribution centre

Topic 1: Activities, Basic | Time: 10 minutes, Core exercise

## Task

At a retailer's distribution centre, deliveries arrive every day. As soon as a truck is at the loading dock, a warehouse employee checks the delivery note. He then unloads the pallets and inspects a sample of the goods. Next he posts the goods receipt in the warehouse management system and puts the goods away. The process ends when the goods are stored. Model the process.

## Learning goal

Model a linear process from the triggering event to the result.

Elements: task, sequence flow, start and end event

## Typical mistakes

- Start event named as an activity instead of a state.
- Two steps merged into one activity ("Unload and inspect pallets").
- Order of posting and storing swapped.
